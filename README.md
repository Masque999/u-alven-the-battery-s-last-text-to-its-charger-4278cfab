# The Battery's Last Text to Its Charger

One line, at most 200 characters: the battery's last text to its charger.

Entry: `Hey, it is me — your battery. I am at 1%, the torch is on, and your human is still scrolling at 3 a.m. If I do not make it, tell the charger it was never its fault.`

Run: `python3 main.py` (writes the sentence to `/output/entry.txt`).
